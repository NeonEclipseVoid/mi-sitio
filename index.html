About me

My name is José Manuel Sáenz Soto. I am a Guatemalan student with ambitious goals, a passion for engineering and motorsport, and a deep belief that success means little if it comes at the cost of your principles.

I want to understand how things work, push my limits through sport, grow in my faith, and build a future that allows me to make a meaningful contribution to the world.

I know the life I envision will demand sacrifice, patience, and discipline. I am prepared to work toward it one day at a time.

More Than Success. A Purpose.

My mission is to become the best version of myself in faith, character, knowledge, and discipline. I want to use my talents to achieve academic excellence, contribute to the world of engineering and motorsport, challenge myself through athletics, and build a family founded on love, loyalty, and faith.

I want to create a life of freedom and opportunity without forgetting where I came from, who stood beside me, or the principles that guide me.

Success Without Compromise.

Faith, family, integrity, loyalty, discipline, perseverance, and service are the principles I want to carry throughout my life.

Faith Before Recognition

My ambitions should never become more important than my relationship with God or the principles I believe in.

Character Before Status

Who I become matters more than what I achieve or what others see. Integrity is the foundation everything else is built on.

Service Before Self

Whatever I earn, I want to use it to lift up my family and the people around me, and never forget who stood beside me.

A life built with purpose.
